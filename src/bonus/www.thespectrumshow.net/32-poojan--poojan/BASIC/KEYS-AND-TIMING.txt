POOJAN — controls shown in the original program

A moves up. Z moves down. SPACE fires.
Any key begins the first stage from the instructions screen.

The replay starts the original Z80 in Fuse 48K mode at 400 percent speed.
It waits 2.5 seconds for startup, then about 20 seconds for the menu
and hall-of-fame screen. It presses SPACE for 0.1 seconds and waits 5 seconds
for the stage display to finish. It holds Z for 0.4 seconds, then A for
0.4 seconds, then SPACE for 0.3 seconds. Screen captures follow each key.
We check that the player's vertical position goes down, then up, and
that the last capture changes after the fire key. This is a brief game
sample, not a completed round.
