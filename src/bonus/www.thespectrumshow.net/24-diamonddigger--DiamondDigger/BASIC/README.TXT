24-diamonddigger--DiamondDigger
The unmodified archived Spectrum 48K snapshot is DiamondDigger.z80; DiamondDigger.tzx is the preserved original tape; the clean 132-line BAS was independently decoded directly from the original TZX with zx-tzx2bas.py.

The BASIC checks 5 for left, 8 for right, 6 for down and 7 for up (lines 220–250). A 500%-speed live Fuse 48K replay was needed for its long startup. The two PNGs are genuine captures of the active colored grid game after right then up; they are not the original archive screenshots. They show moves, not completion.
Run from the repository root: sh src/bonus/www.thespectrumshow.net/24-diamonddigger--DiamondDigger/BASIC/REPLAY.sh (Fuse SDL, Xvfb, python-xlib, Pillow required). bin/ remains empty.
