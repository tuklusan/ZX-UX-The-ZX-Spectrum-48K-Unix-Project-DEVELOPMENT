35-meanmachine--MeanMachine
Original untouched Spectrum 48K snapshot: MeanMachine.z80; original TZX: MeanMachine.tzx. Clean BASIC listing independently decoded directly from original TZX with the existing ROM LLIST converter.

Drive a blue car on a winding track while avoiding oil slicks. Genuine Fuse screen captures show the car on the live track after both driving inputs.
Original listing controls: Code line 133: 8 moves the car right; line 134: 5 moves left; 6 moves down and 7 up. The printed title screen reverses the left/right labels; the executing BASIC is authoritative..
Two PNGs are actual Fuse 48K emulator captures after live keys 8 and 5, not archive screenshots. A short session does not prove winning or a full playthrough.

Reproduce from repository root: sh src/bonus/www.thespectrumshow.net/35-meanmachine--MeanMachine/BASIC/REPLAY.sh. Requires Fuse SDL, Xvfb, Pillow and python-xlib. bin/ remains empty.
