A DAY AT THE RACES — A. G. Bird, 1984

We preserved the original Races.z80 snapshot and the supplied program-specific races.tap and races.tzx without changes. The 123 numbered BASIC lines in races.bas came from the original TZX through the ZX-UX ROM LLIST tool.

The game opens on a title screen. A player starts with 20 in betting money and can choose to bet on one of three horses. We pressed ENTER on the title, chose Y to bet, picked horse 1 and entered a stake of 2. The money dropped to 18 as the animated horse race began. The screenshots are from the real Fuse 48K session. The result and a complete game were not checked.

REPLAY.sh repeats that sequence from the original snapshot. It needs Fuse SDL, Xvfb, the ZX-UX 48K ROM, Python 3, Pillow and python-xlib. No native game file was built.
