DIVER — KEYS AND TIMING

The BASIC source reads 5 for left, 8 for right, 6 for down, and 7 for up. The original snapshot opens on a changing cave field.

From the repository root, run:
  sh src/bonus/www.thespectrumshow.net/29-diver--Diver/BASIC/REPLAY.sh

Fuse SDL 48K runs at 100% speed. We wait 0.6 seconds and press 5, 8, 6, and 7 in that order. Each key stays down for 0.4 seconds, followed by a 0.2 second gap. Screenshots record the screen after each key. The changing cave screen alone does not prove exact player displacement.
