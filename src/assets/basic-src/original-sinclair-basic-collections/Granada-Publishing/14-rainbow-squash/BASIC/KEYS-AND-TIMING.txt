RAINBOW SQUASH — KEYS AND TIMING

We checked the original BASIC and launched the original TAP with Fuse SDL 1.6.0.

Return advances the input prompt for the next ball. The source checks 5 for left and 8 for right while the ball runs. The game can advance between key presses without player input.

The replay waits 18 seconds at normal emulator speed for the color court to load, presses Return for 0.1 seconds, waits 0.4 seconds, holds 5 for 0.6 seconds, then holds 8 for 0.6 seconds. It captures the screen after each direction. The startup duration depends on the emulator and host speed.

The two screenshots show active court changes. They do not establish a complete score or a controlled paddle position.
