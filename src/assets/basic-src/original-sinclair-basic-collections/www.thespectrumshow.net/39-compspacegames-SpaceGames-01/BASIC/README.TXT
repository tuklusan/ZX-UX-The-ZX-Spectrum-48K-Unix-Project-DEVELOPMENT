STARSHIP TAKE-OFF — Computer Spacegames, first of 15 programs

The collection supplies a complete 15-program SpaceGames TAP and TZX and Paul Jenkinson's 2021 Info.txt. We kept those three files unchanged alongside the collection's single-program STARSHIP TAP and TZX. The 19-line BASIC listing was recovered from that single-program TZX with the Spectrum ROM LLIST converter. The book is credited to Usborne Computer Programs, 1982; this media is the later corrected collection, not an original 1982 tape.

The game picks a gravity and a hidden force value. It asks the player to type a force, then says whether the guess is too high, too low, or correct. The listing allows ten guesses (lines 80 through 140). We loaded the supplied single-program tape in Fuse 48K, ran its BASIC, entered 10 and 20, and saw two "TOO HIGH, TRY AGAIN" responses in the captured run. We did not prove a successful take-off. Even in space, guessing is still guessing.

The PNGs in SCREENSHOTS/ came from the live Fuse run. We did not build any other game output. The REPLAY.sh script uses Fuse, Xvfb, the 48K ROM, Python 3, Pillow and python-xlib.
