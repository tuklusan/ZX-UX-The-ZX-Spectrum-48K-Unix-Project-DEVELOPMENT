27-donkeyape--DonkeyApe
Original untouched Spectrum 48K snapshot: DonkeyApe.z80; original TZX: DonkeyApe.tzx. Clean BASIC listing independently decoded directly from original TZX with the existing ROM LLIST converter.

Platform arcade game with a live score, remaining lives, ladders and on-screen ape. Keys were sent during actual gameplay, and the result remained on the live level.
Original listing controls: 8 moves right at line 258; 5 moves left at line 259; 7 climbs at the ladder and 9 jumps.
Two PNGs are actual Fuse 48K emulator captures after live keys 8 and 5, not archive screenshots. A short session does not prove winning or a full playthrough.

Reproduce from repository root: sh src/assets/basic-src/original-sinclair-basic-collections/www.thespectrumshow.net/27-donkeyape--DonkeyApe/BASIC/REPLAY.sh. Requires Fuse SDL, Xvfb, Pillow and python-xlib. bin/ remains empty.
